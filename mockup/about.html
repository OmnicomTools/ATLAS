About OMGI

OMGI: Omnicom Media Global Intelligence is the centralized resource that develops intelligence, investment and innovation strategies for agency teams and clients. We utilize our insights, forecasts and strategic relationships to provide clients with a competitive marketplace advantage.

OMGI harnesses the aggregate power of media investments to create leverage in the market, negotiate preferred pricing and secure premium inventory to drive maximum value for our clients. The OMGI Investment and Innovation teams architect go-to-market investment strategies across all channels including linear television, print, digital and programmatic on behalf of OMC clients. The team focuses on the use of emerging media opportunities, as well as data and technology-enabled solutions to drive optimal client performance and business results.

OMGI Intelligence has set the industry standard for more than 60 years by predicting the future of media value. The OMGI Intelligence team produces more than 40 annual reports on audience trends, media spend and market demand as well as ad effectiveness.

About OMGI Advertising Research

OMGI forecasts have been the industry's leading source for measuring and forecasting advertising revenues. OMGI forecasts media owners' advertising revenues in the US and around the world through the analysis of media companies' public filings, government reports, trade association data and local market expertise. OMGI new methodology was introduced to the industry in 2009 and has redefined measurement for the advertising-supported media economy, delivering unparalleled authority and accuracy.

Our Global Advertising Forecasts analyze television (pay and free), internet (search, display, video, social, mobile), newspapers, magazines, radio, cinema and out-of-home. Our reports monitor and forecast media suppliers' revenues in 70 markets, including all major markets, representing 95% of the world's economy. Our forecasts are updated twice a year and available to OMC employees and external subscribers. Data can be browsed using the OMGI website. Our US Advertising Revenue Forecasts, updated quarterly, include detailed data for more than 40 categories of media on a quarterly basis from 1990 to 2024 and on an annual basis from 1980 to 2030. Other regular OMGI reports on the media industry and the global advertising market place include: the Media Economy Report, the Inflation Report, and the Programmatic Report.

About OMGI Site

The OMGI: Omnicom Media Global Intelligence website is an interactive platform to navigate OMGI's market research on the global advertising market place. It is accessible online, on tablets, and on smartphones. Users can browse and visualize advertising spending data, insights and forecasts for 70 countries, as well as download full OMGI reports.

OMC employees worldwide can use their internal SSO login to access data and reports. Third-party users must request a login or subscription.
